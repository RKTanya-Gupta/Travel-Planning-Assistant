# Hotel Policies (general reference)

Typical policies of Indian hotels, collected by the team for trip planning. The exact policy
can differ, so check with the hotel or booking site before booking.

## Check-in and check-out times
Most hotels in India use a check-in time of 12:00 noon to 2:00 PM and a check-out time of 11:00 AM to 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may be charged, often 50% of the room tariff for up to 6 hours and a full night beyond that.

## Identity documents at check-in
All guests must show a valid government photo ID at check-in, such as an Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence or voter ID. PAN cards are often not accepted as address proof. Foreign nationals must show a passport and a valid Indian visa; hotels are required to register foreign guests with the authorities (Form C).

## Cancellation policy
A common structure is: free cancellation up to 24-72 hours before check-in; a charge of one night's tariff for cancellations inside that window; and no refund for no-shows. Non-refundable rates are cheaper but cannot be cancelled or changed. Peak-season and festival dates (Diwali, Christmas-New Year) often have stricter cancellation rules.

## Child and extra-bed policy
Children up to 5 years usually stay free when using existing bedding. Children 6-12 years may be charged for breakfast or an extra bed. An extra adult bed typically costs 15-25% of the room tariff. Most standard rooms allow a maximum of 2 adults and 1 child.

## Payment, deposits and taxes
Hotels may ask for a refundable security deposit or a card pre-authorisation at check-in. GST is charged on hotel rooms in India and depends on the room tariff band, so the final price can be higher than the displayed base price. Ask whether the quoted price includes taxes.

## Meals and room plans
Room plans are commonly EP (room only), CP (room with breakfast), MAP (breakfast plus one major meal) and AP (all meals). Budget and 3-star hotels often quote EP or CP rates; resorts and hill-station hotels often quote MAP.

## Pets, smoking and visitors
Most Indian hotels do not allow pets unless marked pet-friendly. Smoking is generally prohibited in rooms and public areas; designated smoking zones may exist. Local visitors may be restricted from entering guest rooms for security reasons.

## Star categories
Star ratings indicate facilities: 1-2 star hotels offer basic rooms; 3-star hotels add a restaurant, room service and Wi-Fi; 4-5 star hotels add pools, spas, multiple restaurants, concierge and 24-hour services. This planner maps package tiers to star ranges: Basic 1-3, Standard 2-3, Deluxe 3-4, Super Deluxe 4-5, King 5.
